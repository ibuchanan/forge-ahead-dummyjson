# DummyJSON Contract

Language for maintaining the reviewed DummyJSON contract consumed by Forge examples.

## Language

**Contract verification**:
The assessment that the pinned source routes, reviewed route inventory, committed contract, and generated client types agree on reviewed route semantics.
_Avoid_: Contract check, route check

**Reviewed route inventory**:
The human-reviewed account of the read-only routes and route semantics supported by a specific pinned DummyJSON source revision.
_Avoid_: Route list, endpoint list
